Workflow: Review and suggested improvements.

Critically review the paper as a strong technical reviewer.

Focus on:
- clarity of problem statement and contributions;
- correctness and readability of theorem statements;
- missing definitions or assumptions;
- proof gaps or places where proof structure should be improved;
- related work and citation gaps;
- organization, notation, and narrative;
- concrete edits that would most improve the paper.

Return a LaTeX section with prioritized, actionable suggestions.
